# Exploratory session charter

**Explore** [area or feature]
**with** [resources: data, roles, devices, tools]
**to discover** [the information or risk you are looking for]

Example: *Explore the transfer form with accounts in different currencies and amounts near the daily limit to discover how limits and rounding are validated.*

## Ideas to try

- Limits: zero, negative, maximum, one over the maximum
- Interruptions: refresh, back button, two tabs, lost connection, session timeout
- Order: skip steps, repeat a step, submit twice
- Data: special characters, accents (José Peña), apostrophes (O'Neil), long text, leading spaces
- Roles: a user without permission, a disabled user
- State: what happens to data created halfway through a failed flow
